## // Short bio

I'm fascinated by all things related to [emergence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergence) and [complexity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_system). 
My research focuses on engineering [collective adaptive systems](https://www.unige.ch/cui/cas/).

In this field, I am interested in both manual techniques like [aggregate computing](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/7274429) and multi-agent cooperative reinforcement learning.

Recently, I've started exploring the application of foundational models in software engineering, from requirement management to development.

Beyond research, I'm a huge Scala enthusiast, and in my free time (if it even exists!), I use my Nintendo Switch to unwind and reflect.
