## // Short bio

I'm fascinated by all things related to [emergence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergence) and [complexity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_system). 
This curiosity has led me to pursue a PhD in Computer Science regarding 
 the engineering of [collective adaptive systems](https://www.unige.ch/cui/cas/), where I explored the intersection of distributed systems, 
 multi-agent systems, and machine learning.

Beyond research, 
 I also have a passion for teaching and sharing knowledge.
I still love to explore new technologies and programming languages (in particular Scala), and I enjoy (sometimes) contributing to open-source projects.

Besides my professional interests,
 I'm trying to play the guitar (with limited success),
 play video games (Nintendo Switch is my friend),
 and, when my attention span allows,
 I enjoy reading books outside my field of expertise, especially those related to psychology and physics.
